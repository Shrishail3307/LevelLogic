# Design System: LevelLogic (Cohere-Inspired)

**Project ID:** local-levellogic-01

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The LevelLogic design system is modeled after **Cohere's editorial AI aesthetic**. It functions as a sober enterprise AI command center characterized by extreme restraint, monumental typography, and generous vertical whitespace. The interface alternates between a "White Editorial Canvas" for marketing/data surfaces and "Deep Enterprise Bands" (Green/Navy) for product environments and technical modules.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Brand & Accent
*   **Cohere Black (#000000):** Announcement bars and global brand anchors.
*   **Near-Black Primary (#17171C):** Primary CTA buttons, footer backgrounds, and dark UI cards.
*   **Deep Enterprise Green (#003C33):** Hero bands for technical capability sections and secure environments.
*   **Action Blue (#1863DC):** Links, focus states, and secondary action emphasis.
*   **Coral (#FF7759):** Taxonomy chips, research markers, and warm system status indicators.

### Surface & Background
*   **Canvas White (#FFFFFF):** Dominant page background and primary card surfaces.
*   **Soft Stone (#EEECE7):** Secondary surfaces, hover states, and neutral card blocks.
*   **Pale Blue Wash (#F1F5FF):** Backdrop for secondary insights and specific CTA modules.

### Text & Rules
*   **Ink (#212121):** Default body text and high-contrast typography.
*   **Muted Slate (#93939F):** Metadata, dates, and de-emphasized labels.
*   **Hairline (#D9D9DD):** Standard list rules, section dividers, and thin borders.

## 3. Typography Rules
*   **Display Font:** "Space Grotesk" (Fallback: Inter). Used for massive headlines and section titles.
*   **Body/UI Font:** "Inter". Used for default copy and functional UI text.
*   **Technical Labels:** "JetBrains Mono". Used for uppercase category markers and system status.
*   **Principles:** Headlines use very tight line height (1.0) and negative tracking. Massive scale is used sparingly to create an "editorial declaration" effect.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:** 
    *   **Primary:** Near-black pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with white text.
    *   **Secondary:** Underlined text links or stone-colored pills.
    *   **Outline:** 1px hairline border with transparent fill.
*   **Cards/Containers:**
    *   **Media Cards:** Generously rounded corners (22px) for photography and product mockups.
    *   **UI Cards:** Subtly rounded corners (16px) with thin hairline borders and no drop shadows.
    *   **Grid Layouts:** 8px base grid with large vertical intervals (56px to 96px) between sections.
*   **Inputs/Forms:**
    *   **Editorial Style:** Rectangular with thin 1px bottom border only, or full pill-shaped search bars.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Whitespace Philosophy:** Whitespace is treated as a "trust signal." Large empty intervals separate major brand claims and product proof.
*   **Three-Zone Navigation:** Global headers follow a Logo-Left, Menu-Center, Actions-Right layout.
*   **Surface Alternation:** Depth is achieved through color-blocking (switching from white canvas to dark green bands) rather than shadows or gradients.
